# 01 — Project Brief

## Purpose

Define the business context, website goals, audience, offer, proof logic, scope, and non-goals for the **Parth / Think Big AI Software Systems** microsite.

---

## Project name

**Think Big AI Systems** (microsite) — by **Think Big Digital Solutions**  
Technical founder: **Parth Ghumatkar**

---

## Business / project summary

**Confirmed direction**

This is a serious **AI software systems** vertical under Think Big Digital Solutions. It presents Parth Ghumatkar as a **self-led technical founder** who builds software around real business workflows: internal tools, automation, AI-assisted applications, data platforms, SaaS-style utilities, and content/media pipelines.

Core positioning:

- **AI software systems built around business workflows.**
- **Software first. AI where it improves the workflow.**
- **Proof before claims.**

The site must feel like a **software-company / technical-founder** presence—not a student portfolio, family profile, generic AI agency, or Think Big digital marketing homepage.

---

## Primary audience

- Business owners and founders evaluating custom software or AI-assisted workflow builds
- Operators and consultants who need internal tools, automation, or data systems
- Agencies or companies outsourcing product-style software, dashboards, or workflow applications
- Technical evaluators who will look at **GitHub, demos, and proof documents** before engaging

---

## Website goal

1. **Build trust** through visible, self-built technical proof.
2. **Explain** the AI/software systems direction clearly and calmly.
3. **Route** visitors toward Projects, GitHub, Videos, Proof Library, Downloads, About Parth, and Contact.
4. **Support conversion** to “Discuss a Software Use Case” without hype or fake social proof.

---

## Non-goals

Do **not** build or position as:

- Digital marketing / SEO / lead-gen agency homepage
- Student portfolio or family praise site
- Generic “AI magic” startup landing page
- Fake project grid, fake GitHub/YouTube/download links, fake testimonials or metrics
- Hype copy (genius, prodigy, world-class, revolutionary, etc.)
- Use of the word **“practical”** in visible Parth microsite copy
- Old “Parth Ghumatkar – A Prodigy of Sorts” article tone or content

---

## Success criteria

- Visitor understands **what Parth builds** and **for whom** within 3–5 seconds of the hero.
- Primary CTA is obvious and repeated logically (hero + process + final band).
- Site feels **premium, operational, proof-led**, and aligned with Think Big visual DNA.
- Repeatable content is **CMS-driven** once Phase 3+ is approved—no hard-coded fake cards.
- Responsive layout: no overflow, hero stacks cleanly, readable typography.
- `npm run build` and lint pass before deploy.
- Navigation does not imply broken routes without placeholders or hide-until-ready behavior.

---

## What the site must communicate

- Software systems support **real business workflows**.
- Credibility is **self-built** and **project-led** (GitHub, YouTube, proof library).
- Parth has **full-project ownership**; Think Big adds **review and delivery structure** for serious client work.
- AI is valuable **inside** a system—not as a slogan.

---

## What the site must avoid

- Marketing-agency framing (SEO, campaigns, Growth OS as hero message).
- Over-personal or emotional family narrative.
- Over-emphasis on age; single factual mention in proof context is enough.
- Neon AI visuals, robot/brain stock imagery, fake dashboards.
- Empty or “coming soon” project grids on the public homepage.
